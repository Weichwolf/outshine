**The preparer generates a material and an image**

**Three filed items need one capability that does not exist**, and it has been about to arrive three times
as a side effect of whichever row reached it first. **Building it once, deliberately, is the whole point
of this item.**

[MEASURED] `test/outshine/corpus/prep/fixtures.py` generates subjects through two families — declared shapes, and
`grown.SHAPES` where the engine itself is the producer. **Neither produces a material or an image.** Every
textured case in the tree gets its texture from a **fetched** asset, so every claim that needs a texture
also needs somebody upstream to have authored one.

## Who is waiting, and what each needs

| item | what it needs the fixture for |
|---|---|
| **`board:1179`** | one material, several sockets, **distinct transforms** — the per-reference claim that **no Khronos asset separates** and only a unit test holds |
| **`board:1186`** | a subject of two parts, one carrying `TEXCOORD_1` and one not, the first also with `TANGENT` — the **mixed-part refusal** and **three vertex layouts no corpus asset reaches** |
| **column 2 of `board:0079`** | one crafted glTF per feature, to answer *does a glTF value reach the field* — the column `board:1174` could not measure and priced at **50 reads with no grep substituting for any of them** |

**`board:1186` genuinely cannot start without it and carries the `Depends:`. `board:1179` has a second
path** — leave the claim with its unit test and say so where the corpus count is published — **so it
carries a line in its body and not an edge**, which is this board's rule for a partial block.

## What it must produce, and the constraints are the design

- [ ] **Deterministic bytes across hosts.** The oracle key covers the subject's bytes, so a fixture whose
  image differs by one byte between machines invalidates every case built on it. **A PNG written by an
  explicit loop is reproducible; anything through a library is a dependency question** — and
  `CLAUDE.md` allows no vendored third-party tree
- [ ] **The image is a FUNCTION, not an artwork.** Its purpose is that two samplings of it are
  distinguishable, so it wants high spatial frequency and unambiguous quadrants — the same reason
  `TextureTransformTest` uses an arrow and `MultiUVTest` carries a warning legend. **A gradient proves
  nothing about which texel was read**
- [ ] **Its parameters come from the manifest**, as `fixtures.py` already requires of every shape: *the
  numbers a fixture is built from are the manifest's, not this file's*
- [ ] **It is not a substitute for an upstream asset and must not become one by habit.** A generated
  fixture proves our reading of the specification against our own implementation **with no third party in
  the path** — and `board:1178` is the round where the third party was wrong, which is exactly when that
  independence matters and exactly when it is dangerous

## What it is not

**It is not a general asset authoring capability**, and the scope is the three consumers above. **A
fixture generator that grows a `Lerp` nobody called is the inventory defect this board spent two rounds
undoing**, one layer down.

**Done when** the preparer can emit a subject carrying a material and a deterministic image from declared
parameters, `board:1186`'s two populations are reachable, and column 2 of `board:0079` has an instrument
rather than a price.
