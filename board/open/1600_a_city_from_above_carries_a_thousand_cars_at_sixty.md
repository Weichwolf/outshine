**A city from above carries a thousand cars at sixty**

The owner's picture (2026-08-22): a city filmed from above, roughly a thousand cars moving
through it. One scenario, and it is the forcing function for three open lines at once:

- **presence (1597), first consumer**: the overhead camera is ONE measurement; the traffic
  field over the OSM streets materialises ~1000 individuals at the `rails` rung -- analytic
  corridor-following, no contacts, no minds ticking -- and dissolves them conserving count and
  flow at the frame's edge. Measuring twice yields the same cars (the determinism proof at
  scale).
- **instancing (1538), no way around it**: a thousand F31s recoloured is ONE mesh, ONE draw
  with a real instance count and per-instance placements -- the literal 1 cannot survive this
  scenario.
- **the frame floor**: 720p60 held as p50/p95/p99 over a moving overhead camera, on the
  scenario suite's own terms -- the fourth constraint judged over a picture worth looking at.

Acceptance: a declared scenario (city coordinates, camera height, clock) stands up through the
one door; the suite publishes car count materialised, field count represented, conservation
residue (cars leaked at the seam = 0), the frame distribution, and a still a person can open.
Traffic law is not modelled; the field's fundamental diagram and the corridor speed plan are.
