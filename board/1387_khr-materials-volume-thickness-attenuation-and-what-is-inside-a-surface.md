**Khr materials volume thickness attenuation and what is inside a surface**

Thickness, attenuation distance and attenuation colour -- the inside of a transmissive body, which is
what makes thick glass green at its edge and a gemstone read as a solid rather than a shell. **It has no
meaning without transmission**, so it depends on that task rather than merely following it.

**One model at the pin requires it**; twenty-five use it.

Specification: <https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/tree/main/extensions/2.0/Khronos/KHR_materials_volume>. **Fetched, never recalled** -- a rule quoted from memory is a defect one step before the code.
