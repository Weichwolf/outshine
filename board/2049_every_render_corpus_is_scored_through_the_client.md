# Every render corpus is scored THROUGH THE CLIENT, against a hash that was made once

**Benchmark** — Unreal: the automation framework drives the SHIPPING binary and compares a
screenshot against a committed reference; the reference is produced once and reviewed by an eye,
never regenerated by the thing under test. RAGE: the same shape, `rag` drives the build and the
golden images are content. **They agree**, so the matter is closed -- what is open is that this
tree does not do it.

## What stands today

Six vendor scorers, each a `.cpp` linked against the library and reaching PAST the door:

    the two render scorers                                     deleted; see below
    test/harness/khronos/validator/ScoreEveryValidatorCase.cpp <Outshine.h> -- the DOOR already
    test/harness/wpt/css/ScoreEveryWptCase.cpp                 Layout.h Markup.h Style.h
    test/harness/test262/js/ScoreEveryScriptCase.cpp           Script.h
    test/harness/geographiclib/geodesic/ScoreEveryGeodesic.cpp Geodesy.h Wgs84.h

The last three score a COMPUTATION and stay direct: forcing a geodesic through a command line adds
a serialisation nobody asked for and proves nothing about the door. **The first two DRAW, and a
render corpus that reaches past the door proves nothing about the product.** That is this item.

## What will be true

- [ ] `outshine-client` gains the verbs the render corpus needs, and the two render scorers become
      SCRIPTS that run the client and read what it prints
- [ ] each model's frame carries a digest, the digests are committed as a file, and a ROLL-UP hash
      of all of them is one line -- the roll-up is the gate, the file is the diagnosis
- [ ] the Blender oracle runs ONCE, its hash is committed, and no test run re-derives it
- [ ] a verb the corpus needs and the door cannot serve is a FINDING against the door, filed as
      its own item rather than worked around in the script

## Why the door and not the library

The standing goal is a complete door. A corpus that links `src/` can be green while the door
cannot open a single one of those files, and this tree has already paid for that exact trap once
(`a gate blind to a path`, CLAUDE.md). Scoring 148 Khronos models through `outshine-client` makes
the corpus the door's own completeness test: every gap shows up as a verb that does not exist.

## What this does NOT cover

The four claims and 29 harness claim cases are not in this. Neither is `test/run.sh`, which is
2340 lines and mostly per-suite include paths and link flags that this item deletes the need for --
that is its own item and it comes after, when there is less of it left.
