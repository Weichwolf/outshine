# OSM infrastructure stands on the displaced sphere

**Benchmark** — Unreal: an actor is placed by transform and its Z comes from a landscape trace. RAGE: map objects carry a placement the map node authored, and the terrain is authored to match. **Taking Unreal** because outshine's terrain is FETCHED rather than authored: nothing can pre-agree with a raster that arrives at runtime, so a building asks the ground where it stands.

STEP THREE OF THREE, and it is where the five places stop being terrain and start being places.
Buildings and streets, no vegetation -- the owner's bound, because a placement worth looking at
does not exist yet.

## What will be true

- [ ] a footprint becomes a body standing on the displaced sphere, its base the ground beneath its centre
- [ ] a way becomes a ribbon following the ground along its length
- [ ] the instances reach the picture through ONE instanced draw per class, N instances, N rows
- [ ] no vegetation is placed at any of the five places

## The measurements that would show I am wrong

1. `instances its draw sources made` and `building footprints it holds` converge on the same subject rather than on each other's fractions
2. A building's base must equal `sampleHeight` at its centre to within the tile grid -- if it floats or sinks, the ground it asked was not the ground that drew
3. Rothenburg's street count and its drawn ribbon length must stand in the ratio OSM's own way lengths give, computed from the raw features rather than from what was drawn
